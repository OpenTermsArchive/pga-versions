European Ad Information & Options
=================================

The [Digital Services Act ("DSA")](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/digital-services-act-package) is a European Union (EU) law that requires online services like Twitch to provide a number of safety and transparency-related features to EU residents. As required by the DSA, Twitch provides enhanced ad information and options for users in the EU.

Ad Information
--------------

Twitch advertisements have an “Ad ⓘ” icon prominently displayed when playing. This disclosure is designed to notify you that the content is an ad, as well as to provide you with additional information and options, when the icon is clicked. (Note that [Branded Content](https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/branded-content-policy) is identified by the “Includes Paid Promotion” disclosure, rather than the “Ad ⓘ” icon.)

If you are viewing an ad from the EU, clicking on the “Ad ⓘ” icon will open a window with information about why you’re being shown the ad, the name of the advertiser, and who paid for the ad.

Ad Preferences
--------------

From the “About this advertisement” window you can click “Go to advertisement preferences” to be redirected to an Amazon site where you can see and change your ad preferences. To learn more about your choices when it comes to Twitch advertising, please visit our [Ads Choices](https://www.twitch.tv/p/legal/ad-choices/) page.

Ad Feedback
-----------

If you would like to provide feedback on the Twitch ad you were viewing, click “Leave advertisement feedback.” A feedback window will open where you can let us know if the ad is relevant, appropriate, helpful, and more. We’ll use your feedback to improve your ad experience on Twitch.

Ad Reporting & Reporter Appeals
-------------------------------

EU residents may also report suspected illegal content found in an ad by clicking “Report this advertisement” in the feedback window. You will then be redirected to an Amazon site where you can submit your report.

Once a decision has been made on your report, AmazonAds will send you an email informing you of the decision and providing instructions on how you can appeal the decision.

Once your appeal has been decided, AmazonAds will send you an email informing you of the decision. If you wish to contest the decision further, you may be able to utilize other legal methods for seeking redress, including the out-of-court dispute settlement process established by the DSA.
